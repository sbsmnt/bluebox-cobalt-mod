Unofficial effects & theme mod for bluebox desktop firmware 1.5.2 · v__VERSION__

Runs locally · no networkOriginal firmware never leaves your device

Use the original official desktop 1.5.2 firmware. Includes the Gazer reverb (FX2 → B → Gazer), Gate with MIXER detector meters, FX1 modes (Delay, Granular, Tape with Lo C, Ducked, Reverse), Soft/Tape master saturation, the CPU load bar, compressor meters and the overdub recording fix. Keep your working firmware and a project backup.

Runs locally · no networkOriginal firmware never leaves your deviceNew in v1.3 ›Scenes and MIDI guide ›

Development build from the dev branch: the next Cobalt version while it is tested on hardware. It adds synth tracks, track inserts (DRV, FLTR, SPEC), the DJ EQ and filter, an analog clock on Out 2 and User Settings to Cobalt v1.2. TOOLS shows Cblt 1.3d. Keep your working firmware and back up your projects. For the current release use v1.2.0.
